# L’urlo e il furore — two realistic illustrated maps

Implemented 15 October 2026, following the user's request for two separate realistic panoramas, one of the Compson property and one of Jefferson. These are map backgrounds; the catalogue retains Winslow Homer's existing painting.

## Textual and cartographic grounding

The complete reading and original-text verification are recorded in `analysis.json`, `verification.json` and the book's existing editorial record. The images add no literary excerpts: the runtime reads the existing verified originals, translation availability, exact highlight terms and attribution from Supabase. Pending or rejected originals are not promoted to image markers.

The first section's fence and golf passage supports the former pasture; the garden opening, gate, barn, stream and swing have their own original section evidence. These support contextual readings, not the generated architecture or street geometry. Primary text URLs and editions remain in the existing analysis, not in public quotation links.

## Image anchors and categories

`panorama.json` stores the two independent image anchor sets. Image coordinates are normalized fractions and never enter terrestrial latitude/longitude fields. The property has eight markers: house, pasture, garden, gate, cabin, barn, branch, swing. Jefferson has five: square, monument, store, cemetery and the church of the Black congregation. Interior rooms and other settings with no independently visible feature remain in the source analysis. Markers must not borrow positions from the other image.

Categories belong to section–place occurrences. The display retains published mentions and conservatively uses `mentioned` for II/square and swing, III/store and square, and appendix/cabin and house, because these passages establish a view, invitation, employment, comment, residence or reported sale rather than a scene at that setting. I/swing retains action following the contextual passage in which Benjy sees the swing and cries. The appendix store scene retains action because Melissa enters the shop. Original imported records are retained separately; no quotation is changed to justify a category.

The shared controller automatically selects the image containing the chosen place and offers an explicit two-view selector. Real places keep their original coordinates and use the geographical basemap; Quentin's section opens there. The return button leads back to the image. Section introductions and the distinct appendix are retained. `sourceChapters` preserves the original evidence and geography for unchanged CSV exports; no image pixel coordinate is exported as WGS84.

## Publication and checks

The configuration, labels, place names and critical notes in EN/IT/FR/ES are stored in `books.metadata.illustratedView`; `publish-panorama.sql` updates only that key. The catalogue and its map-type routing remain unchanged: the normal dataset is loaded, then the generic `illustrated-overlay.js` creates an immutable display view. The local `docs/data` copy is a backup only.

Tests cover source immutability, literal quotation reuse, pending originals, locale completeness, original CSV equality, two independent position sets, automatic image choice, layer numbering, selected identity and idempotent SQL that preserves other books and metadata. Browser QA covers both panoramas, citations, geographical navigation, view switching, section/global selection and mobile layout. Existing Macondo controller and atlas tests also run.
